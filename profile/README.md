👋 Hi there ! We are the research team of the [Chair Computational Mathematics](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/cm) 
from the [Math Institute](https://www.mat.tuhh.de) of [Hamburg University of Technology (TUHH)](https://www.tuhh.de/tuhh/en/startpage).

Our research topics cover the development and analysis of numerical methods for numerical simulations in science and engineering, in particular

- [Machine Learning methods for Parallel-in-time algorithms](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/Machine_Learning_For_PINT.html)
- [Parallel-in-time algorithms for bathymetry reconstruction with SWE](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/Parallel-in-time_algorithms_for_shallow_water_equations.html)
- [Parallel-in-Time PDE-constrained optimization](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/PinT-PDEopt.html)
- [Parallel-in-Time methods for Initial Value Problems](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/PinT.html)
- [Spectral deferred correction methods for second-order problems](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/SDC.html)
- [An efficient numerical method for the Maxey-Riley Equation](https://www.mat.tuhh.de/forschung/topics/The_Maxey-Riley_Equation.html)
